Light Sensors

Purpose:
The purpose of this is to measure ambient light levels and automatically control the led based on brightness.

Necessary Devices:
Esp32 Dev board, LDR, resistor, LED, Breadboard, Jumper wires, USB cable.

Steps:
1-Connect the LDR and resistor as a voltage divider and link it to esp32 analog pin
2-Connect the LED to a digital pin with a resistor
3-Set up the ESP32
4-Set a brightness threshold
5-Turn the LED on or off depending on the level of light
